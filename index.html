FAQ

Questions employers often ask.

Can PREMIER HR support work pass applications?

We can assist with work pass-related preparation and coordination based on employer requirements and applicable rules.

What should employers prepare first?

Prepare the role scope, salary range, working schedule, preferred start date and any work pass expectations.

What can the AI robots help with?

They can support reminders, enquiry collection, FAQ replies, contact capture and handoff to human staff.

Is PREMIER HR a MOM department?

No. PREMIER HR is a private employment agency. MOM information should be checked through official MOM channels when needed.
